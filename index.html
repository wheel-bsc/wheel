💡 Good to know

Send more than the cost and the excess comes back in the same transaction. Every spin's price splits in the same transaction: 70% to the treasury and 30% into the prize pot — and when the game ends, the pot is paid out 85% to players by points and 15% to the treasury.

📜 How It Works

  1. The game: call spin() for 1 to 10 spins per transaction. Every spin costs 0.0005 BNB. The game runs for exactly 3 days from deployment; after that, spinning closes forever.
  2. The split: 70% of every spin's price goes to the treasury instantly, 30% goes into the prize pot — and at the end, the pot itself is paid out 85% to the players by points and 15% to the treasury (see The ending).
  3. Points & tokens: every point mints 10,000 WHEEL to your wallet on the spot. WHEEL is minted only as spin rewards — hard cap 1 billion (1,000,000,000).
  4. The JACKPOT face: face 99 counts as 10 points (100,000 WHEEL) and also pays you 10% of the pot instantly (counted after that spin's own 30% lands).
  5. The ending: after 3 days anyone can call finalize(). The remaining pot splits: 85% to the players, claimed pro-rata by total points via claim() (one claim per player), and 15% to the treasury. If nobody scored any points, the whole pot goes to the treasury.
  6. Fair by code: every spin's face is derived on-chain from the previous block's hash. No admin, no owner, no changes — ever.
